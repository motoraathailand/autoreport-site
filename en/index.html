What is AutoReport?

An app for garages and mechanics that keeps all the paperwork in one place. It starts with car repair; more business types will follow.

Quotes

Add parts, labour and tax and see the total at once. Send a PDF to the customer or print it on A4.

Job sheets

When the customer agrees, turn the quote into a job sheet in one tap. Set Waiting / In progress / Done from any screen.

Service history

Search by licence plate to see past work, odometer, before/after photos, and set a next-service reminder.

Key features

A4 print and PDF

Quotes and job sheets are laid out neatly on A4. Print them or share as PDF.

Your logo and shop details

Shop name, logo, address and tax ID on every document.

155 currencies and tax

Pick your currency and tax type, such as 7% VAT or sales tax, to match your country.

Kilometres or miles

Record the odometer in the unit your shop uses.

Read plate and odometer from a photo

Take a photo and the app reads the characters on the phone itself. The photo is not sent anywhere, and you can correct it before saving.

Offline first

Work without internet. When you are back online, the app syncs to Drive.

Google sign-in

Data and PDFs are saved in the “AutoReport” folder in your own Google Drive, so you can restore them on a new phone.

Tablet support

The layout adapts to the screen, so it is comfortable on phones and tablets.

5 languages

Thai, English, Korean, Chinese and Hindi, switchable on every screen.

FAQ

Where is my data? Is it safe?

On your phone, with a backup in the “AutoReport” folder in your own Google Drive. The app asks only for the Drive “drive.file” permission, which can see just the files AutoReport created, never your other files. We do not run a server that stores your business data. See the privacy policy for details. Privacy policy

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Make quotes and job sheets and read history as usual; when you are back online the app syncs to Google Drive by itself. (You sign in with Google once, the first time you open the app.)

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Are there ads?

There are no ads during this closed test. After the official release there may be a small banner only, which you can scroll past. There will be no full-screen ads.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings > Google account > “Delete my Drive data”. Or see the Delete account and data page. Delete account and data

Which devices work?

Android phones and tablets, installed from Google Play.
